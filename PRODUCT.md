# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

SERENA serves style-led jewellery shoppers in India, especially young customers buying for themselves, friends, birthdays, college looks, reels, and small gifting moments. They browse quickly on mobile, want the jar to feel personal, and need a clear path to order through WhatsApp or Instagram rather than a conventional website checkout.

## Product Purpose

SERENA sells mystery jewellery jars curated around a customer's vibe, budget, colour mood, and preferred jewellery types. The website should make the curation feel special, show the available jar drops clearly, and guide visitors toward saving jars or starting a WhatsApp/Instagram order with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Personal, tactile, and reveal-driven. The brand should feel like a small jewellery studio packing a thoughtful surprise: intimate enough to message directly, polished enough to trust, and expressive enough for gifting and social sharing.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic beige boutique templates, overloaded emoji decoration, vague online-checkout language, repeated tiny section eyebrows, glassmorphism-heavy cards, and anything that makes the brand feel like a faceless marketplace. The site should not imply payment is collected on the website.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the jar ritual: make the mystery, curation, and reveal obvious in the first viewport.
- Keep ordering honest: use "Save to Order", "Confirm via WhatsApp", and direct message language.
- Let products do the work: show jar names, moods, prices, stock urgency, and included categories without decorative clutter.
- Design mobile-first: customers should be able to scan, save, and message without horizontal scrolling or cramped controls.
- Make it personal: reinforce that Manyata and Hridyanshi curate the jars directly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced motion, keep tap targets comfortable on mobile, avoid essential information conveyed only by colour or animation, and keep WhatsApp/Instagram order paths available as clear text links.
